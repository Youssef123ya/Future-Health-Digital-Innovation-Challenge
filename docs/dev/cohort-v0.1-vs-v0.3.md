Version notes (scripts/validate/compare-cohorts.ts VERSION_NOTES):

- World simulation (simulated world: mechanics, schedules) 1.1.0: wave 2c: structured truth events, supervisor follow-through, consent changes on the device, ADOSH hourly dose in the site TWL index, idle minutes, plan partial windows; a followed drink reminder replaces that minute’s spontaneous drink.
- World simulation (simulated world: mechanics, schedules) 1.2.0: engine-owned resume (projection hold), the day plan’s ISO 7933 screen on the forecast climate, plan block-hour execution layout, device work/rest cycles aligned with plan block-hours.
- World simulation (simulated world: mechanics, schedules) 1.3.0: wave 2d: the listed-order schedule (midday ban only, site TWL index, shadow) honours task windows like the planner — a baseline definition change (no work before the windows open, work left when a window closes is deferred, idle minutes count toward the statutory break); the worker responds to the reminder the device shows (the engine’s rule: rest and idle in heat included); drink-logging fidelity (default: every drink logged exactly).
- World simulation (simulated world: mechanics, schedules) 1.4.0: wave 2d (integration): members on an acclimatisation schedule follow their own per-member plan caps (the others the crew layout; floored bouts); the monitored-mode policy (zill_monitored: planner and devices on the monitored plan basis); the engine owns every resume decision (the simulator’s own check of the task to resume is gone). Baseline arms are not touched by this version.
- Ground-truth model (simulated world: physiology, behaviour) 1.1.0: wave 2c drinking model: sweat-paced spontaneous drinking with a habitual floor of 0.4 × the individual baseline (it was a 450 mL/h floor).
- Ground-truth model (simulated world: physiology, behaviour) 1.2.0: wave 2d: habitual drinking floor calibrated to the observed hot-shift intake (5.44 L per 12-h shift, Bates & Schneider 2008): 0.4 → 0.87; a drink reminder left on screen is followed at most once per 20 min.
- Metric definitions 1.1.0: alarms split by basis: headline detection on individual alarms (≥ high and ≥ stop), exposure advisories reported separately, alert burden by basis and role.
- Metric definitions 1.2.0: designed suppression (ANSI/ISA-18.2): an alert version logged under designed suppression is not an alarm or an exposure advisory; suppressed advisories are counted per worker-shift.
- Back-test design and report schema 1.1.0: supervisor follow-through variant; ground-truth parameter sweeps run.
- Back-test design and report schema 1.2.0: arms from one table; drinking-floor (1.0, 0.4) and drink-logging (50 %, ×1.5) variants; base-case delta on each variant’s site-days; report versions.truth.
- Back-test design and report schema 1.3.0: arms zill_monitored (monitored plan basis) and shadow_zill_monitored; headline suppressed advisories and supervisor acknowledgements per worker-shift.
